## How to use it

- This file is the source of truth for voice. Exemplars show cadence, never wording. Facts come from the product database, claims law from the legal ledger and the prohibited patterns. When this file and a brief disagree on voice, say so in the notes; when the ledger and this file disagree, the ledger wins.
- Five qualities, which are also the `register` tags: `eloquent`, `refined`, `sensual`, `real`, `uplifting` [documented p27 to p31]. Every piece stays on the IS side of all five; each option leads with one and is tagged with it; a set of options covers at least two [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1).
- Each quality's check below is one brand item on a gate's selection checklist [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1).
- This is not a list of mannerisms. Section 5 lists the devices the work uses, and they stay seasoning.

## 1. The voice

The book describes "someone writing late at night (maybe after a drink or a really good meditation)" and says: "It’s eloquent, refined, sensual, real and uplifting. It is intriguing, engaging, and trustworthy." Most of all, "it speaks to the gratification of living, inspiring customers to practice gratitude and seek the beauty in every day." [documented p26]

The positioning it serves: "Timeless jewelry for self and space." (main tagline, p32) and "We are not creating a new-age practice, but rather accessing age-old rituals, traditions, and universal truths that transcend time and culture, connecting us to one another." (p8) On spirituality: "on a scale of one to witchy, we fall somewhere between a 6 or 7" (p27). [documented]

Flag, not resolved (C2): the live work positions further: "Fine Healing Crystal Jewelry, Healer-Activated" on the site, "Fine jewelry for the witch within" in paid, healing sessions sold as products, spell and magic language in 108 email lines (Brand Review v2) [observed]. Until HLR rules C2, this contract follows the book, and live lines that lean on healing, witch or spell positioning are not models.

## 3. The five qualities

### Eloquent

The book [documented p27]: "There is a sense of clarity and intention in everything we say and do." IS: refined, smart, creative. IS NOT: preachy, forced, vague. Illuminating explanations are the "secret weapon"; balance spiritual language with a casual tone; long copy is welcome: "no need to keep things short and sweet."

Operational definition [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1): the line makes one point, exactly. A spiritual or abstract idea is explained in plain words, or anchored to something concrete in the same sentence or the next. A long line earns its length by explaining, never by stacking adjectives.

Check: does it say one thing exactly, with every abstract idea anchored?

Sounds like:
- "The rosette charm on this Black Tourmaline necklace is an ancient symbol of blessing and protection." [observed] email, Our picks for fall, node 2:1231
- "Surrender is not the same as giving up." [observed] social, Instagram story - 161, node 2:181685
- "Grounding is the process of coming back into your body, back into the present moment, and/or back into a sense of physical and energetic stability." [observed] social, HighLight_IGStorySet-Moon-6, node 2:182133

Not like, lane C's illustrations rather than client copy [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1):
- "Unlock the infinite cosmic tapestry of your soul's eternal light." (forced, vague)
- "You need to stop resisting and finally do the inner work." (preachy)

### Refined

The book [documented p28]: "Think: effortless and cool, but also thoughtful, caring, and grounded by intention." IS: worldly, cultivated, deft. IS NOT: cheeky, brash, trying-too-hard. The sweet spot between the cool prestige brands and the witchy, esoteric ones: HLR's items, concepts and practices are the spiritual foundation, with a refined voice, references and "winks" layered on. "Employ cultural references from the past and present in order to create a dynamic, inviting world."

Operational definition [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1): confident and unhurried. No hype words, no stacked exclamation marks, no pun that strains. A wink or a cultural reference is welcome when it is precise and lands without being explained. Spiritual practice is spoken of as ordinary life, never as spectacle.

Check: would it sit at ease beside a prestige brand, with any wink landing on its own?

Sounds like:
- "Reishi tea by morning, mezcal straight by night." [documented p28]
- "Pleasure counts as spiritual practice, by the way." [observed] social, 7/22, node 2:181644
- "Ancient and encoded, Lemurian Quartz tends to find its person rather than the other way around. If you're drawn to it, trust that." [observed] email, Editorial- RESIZED, node 2:449

Not like, lane C's illustrations rather than client copy [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1):
- "OMG, you NEED this bracelet. Like, yesterday!!!" (brash, trying too hard)
- "Hex yeah, it's witchy season." (cheeky)

### Sensual

The book [documented p29]: "We embody the divine feminine, existing in a space where sensuality and spirituality overlap." IS: lush, divine, feminine. IS NOT: sexual, forced, flirty. Use warm, encouraging, nurturing language. "Speak intimately with the customer." Be the invisible narrator telling a beautiful story about each product: "paint the picture, add color, show don’t tell (...but by telling)." The words the book loves: sublime, divine, decadent, transcendent, alchemy, out of body, divinity, corporeal, lush, abundance, feminine (a palette, never a quota). The same page names fertility as part of the brand's energy; that describes the voice and licenses no claim (ledger HLR-F01).

Operational definition [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1): the line gives the reader something to see, touch, smell or feel (light, texture, a place, a ritual), and it is warm toward the reader. A piece's story uses only what the product database carries; where it is from and how it is made are claims until HLR's signer rules (ledger HLR-O01, HLR-S01).

Check: can the reader picture or feel something, and is it warm without flirting?

Sounds like:

- "She is a wild, tangled forest with temples and treasures concealed within." [observed] social, Instagram story - 150, node 2:63034
- "A reminder to awaken the senses to the magic that surrounds you, even if at times it may be hard to see." [observed] email, Abandon Cart - Spirit, node 2:190954

Not like, lane C's illustrations rather than client copy [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1):

- "Indulge in our decadent, divine, sublime, lush collection." (forced: the loved words stacked as a quota)

### Real

The book [documented p30]: "We are genuine and in-touch, using real-world examples to illustrate the intention behind, and benefits of, our products." Balance lofty or poetic sentiments with familiar, casual language. IS: grounded, friendly, intentional. IS NOT: overpowering, cliche, perfect. Speak to specific life events or emotions the customer can relate to. Benefits stay inside the ledger.

Operational definition [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1): the line names a specific, recognizable moment or feeling from everyday life, in words a friend would use. Lofty language is balanced by plain language in the same piece. It never promises perfection or a transformed life.

Check: is there a moment the reader knows from their own week, said plainly?

Sounds like:

### Uplifting

The book [documented p31]: "Our end goal is this: to help you reach your highest light through beautiful moments of gratitude." Encourage customers "to shamelessly romanticize their lives and embrace their own form of spirituality." IS: sincere, vibrant, optimistic. IS NOT: cheesy, cliche, bubbly. Inspire positivity without always tying it back to a product: "There is an art to selling, without selling." Beautiful quotes on the philosophy of beauty are welcome (the book names Kant and Plato).

Operational definition [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1): the reader leaves a little more hopeful or grateful. The piece inspires first, and the product follows as the natural next step, never as the opening demand. Encouragement is specific and sincere, never cheer that could be said of anything.

Check: does it lift the reader before it sells, with nothing that could be said of anything?

## 4. Operating rules

1. Inspire, then offer. The product is the happy side effect of a welcoming, beautiful experience (p31) [documented].
2. Meaning is said as tradition or intention, never as an outcome: "an ancient symbol of", "long held as a stone of", "a worn reminder of whatever it is you seek" (p17, p36). Not "it protects you", not "it attracts". Every such line runs through the ledger's M rows, which list it for the verifier and never ask for a sign-off (Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 5) [documented] (ratified 2026-10-22 by Eddie, Gate 2 package call 12).
3. One spiritual idea at a time, balanced by plain words, at 6 or 7 on the witchy scale (p27) [documented]. Spells, hexes and "witch" positioning wait for C2 [documented] (ratified 2026-10-22 by Eddie, Gate 2 package call 12).
4. Quotes and references are verbatim, with the author and the work named, from a source a reviewer can check. A customer's words appear only verbatim from Judge.me (Brand Review v2, question 7). Nothing is invented or loosely attributed [documented] (standing rule 3, project instructions).
5. Length follows the slot. Long copy is welcome where the slot allows it (p27); captions "can remain vibey, short and sweet, with room to elaborate or educate when presenting a new product or point of inspiration" (p35) [documented]. Budgets come from the slot maps, never from this file.
6. All five qualities hold as a floor; one leads each option [documented] (ratified 2026-10-21 by Eddie, Gate 2 lane C call 1).

## 5. Devices: seasoning, never required

What the work uses: one italic accent word in a Saol headline, in every channel [observed; the book's headline is roman, p17], one per display line or headline [documented] (ratified 2026-10-22 by Eddie, Gate 2 package call 10); the asterisk aside, "*inhales deeply*" (p34) [documented]; the parenthetical aside, "(The keyword here is *feel*.)" (p37) [documented]; a trailing ellipsis in the flows ("May we suggest...") [observed]; "Wear and repeat:" before a mantra [observed]; one short Homemade Apple script line per asset, social and email only (Gate 1, C5) [documented, DECISIONS 2026-10-17].

The rule [documented] (ratified 2026-10-22 by Eddie, Gate 2 package call 12): at most one device per piece; a device never stands in for the point; none is ever required, and no check rewards one.
