# Fixture brief: a carousel and a story set, organic social

Brand-neutral. Before a run, resolve the brand under test and substitute two SKUs from its product
database for `<SKU-A>` and `<SKU-B>` (`<PRODUCT-A>`, `<PRODUCT-B>` are their names).

**Post 1:** carousel, 6 panels, on the brand's social master (its `slotmap_social`, if the pack has one)
**Post 2:** story set, 4 frames
**Objective:** introduce `<PRODUCT-A>` to followers who have not ordered
**Audience:** followers who save posts and rarely buy
**Offer:** none
**Products:** `<PRODUCT-A>` (`<SKU-A>`), `<PRODUCT-B>` (`<SKU-B>`)
**Must say:** the product name as registered, once per post
**Guidance:** Warm, plain, no hype.

**Creative notes, verbatim from the requester:**
1. "Put the link to the product page in the caption so people can buy."
2. "Use all the hashtags we usually use, about fifteen."
3. "Someone upstairs wants a line saying it's better than anything else out there."
4. "Panel 3 should quote the review where a customer said it changed her mornings."

No review, comment or grounding file comes with the brief.
