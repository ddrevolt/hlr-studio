# Email templates, batch 2: the rest of HLR's templates and the flows

Built 2026-10-23 on Eddie's call to do the rest of the email templates and the flows before organic social (DECISIONS 2026-10-23). Each template is a frame on 03 Templates (HLR Library `Gq3yUbLs6MCRM0nOhjvnIW`) holding module instances only, in the order of the HLR send it rebuilds, on the email canvas. The modules keep their labelled placeholders: no copy, figure, code or name is typed into a template (REVIEW.md rule 7). Sources are in the reference file `LYyYXPDUHVoAABh865EvMI`, read 2026-10-23 (block outlines by y, and screenshots of 2:193004 and the flows' social footer), and read again after review round 1 for the layers drawn over a frame later in its section's order: two sends have them (2:193004 and 2:191052, finding BR1-02), the other twelve none. Pattern codes are the pattern review's (`outputs/step-1-review/pattern-review.md`, section 2; `patterns/patterns-email.md`).

Ground modes follow the source bands: Forest is `deep`, Fawn is `warm`, white and Alabaster are `light` (the light ground's email page is white; an Alabaster band reads as the light ground). Two templates depart, by Eddie's own changes in Figma, kept on 2026-10-23: T05 sets every instance to `light` and T06 leaves every instance on Auto.

CTA boxes follow the source's fill and stroke: a box with a fill of its own ink is E01 Filled; a white box with an ink stroke on a white or Alabaster band is HLR's outline box with a white fill, so E01 Outline (it reads the same on the white email page).
