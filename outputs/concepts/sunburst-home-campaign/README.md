# Sunburst home campaign (concept round)

2026-10-23, Eddie's request in chat: a ten-image campaign with the gold sunburst as a small decorative object for the home, made in Adobe Firefly through Claude in Chrome. Concept only: whether a generated image may stand in for a real shot is not ruled for HLR (`brand-pack/art-direction.md` section 4), so nothing here ships until that is ruled.

## Eddie's calls in this chat

1. Solid and dimensional, resting low on its long arm, not standing tall.
2. Striations along the arms; arms uneven, none identical; a fuller center.
3. Master: in between his two picks (the domed eight-arm star and the needle starburst), loaded by Eddie as the reference.
4. Gold: the pendant's texture and color, not its design.
5. Look: very simple, a black onyx countertop, short focus, directional but soft light, a low white point even where the light has an edge.

Superseded on the way: bright home scenes (read as a catalog, not HLR today) and dark ritual still lifes (replaced by the onyx set).

## Master, gold finish (run on Eddie's master as the reference)

```
Edit the reference image: keep the sculpture exactly as it is, same shape, arms, center, striations and pose, and keep the same framing, background and light. Change only the gold finish: warm butter-yellow 14k gold with a soft satin surface, a fine and even micro-grain like bead-blasted fine jewelry, a diffused glow instead of mirror reflections, and bright polished highlights only along the sharpest edges and tips. The color is warm and slightly muted, not orange, not brassy, not pale. No text, no other objects.
```
