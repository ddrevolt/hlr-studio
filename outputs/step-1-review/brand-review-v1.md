## 2. Contradictions (flagged, not resolved)

- **C1 Name.** The book says HIGH LIGHT; the marks and posts since mid-2023 say High Light Rituals and HLR. Decide the name in copy: full form, short form, caps.
- **C2 Marks.** The book's shell system against the 2024 set (shell with star, HLR with star, star, crest). Decide the current roster and its rules; p14's "shell only beside the logotype" may no longer hold.
- **C3 Black.** Onyx #353535 (book, 2022 marks) against #231f20 (2024 logo SVGs). Decide which black is law.
- **C4 Faces.** Instrument Serif and Homemade Apple (social) and GT Walsheim Pro and Cannu (font folder) appear in no document. Decide each face's role, or retire it.
- **C5 Tagline.** "Timeless jewelry for self and space" (p32) against "Sanctuaries of self and space" (p15, p51 to p53). Decide the current line and where each one lives.
- **C6 Em dashes.** The book's copy uses them throughout; our house rule removes them from everything we make. The client should hear this from us.
- **C7 Book against live templates.** Unknown until the copies are readable.
