# Crew checks

Every check is a file-reading CLI: it takes `--output <stage>.json` (and `--pack`, default `brand-pack/`), prints `ERROR` lines and a verdict, and exits 0 on PASS or 1 on FAIL. `run.py` runs the one named in each co-worker's `prompt.md` front matter and stops on a non-zero exit. Standard library only; `common.py` holds the shared pieces (the File map reader, the prohibited-pattern scan, the `slotmap.yaml` text parser, the bundle locator).

The engine's own lints (`brief_lint.js`, `copy_lint.js`, `paid_lint.js`) live in the newest `hlr-studio-engine-*.plugin` under `outputs/step-3-bundle/`. They read the Phase 1 shapes (the engine's brief schema, the route plan, the email and paid slates). None of them can read the crew's JSON yet, so each wrapper prints one `SKIPPED engine <lint> (<bundle>): <reason>` line and runs the crew-native checks instead. A SKIPPED line is not a pass: it names what has not been proven.

| Check | Command | What it proves | What it does not prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| intake | `python3 crew/checks/intake_check.py --output <run>/intake.json` | resolved product ids exist in `products.json` with their display names; unresolved products have a gap; every gap has an owner; `never_say` carries every pack prohibited pattern; no em dash, prohibited hit, typed price or typed date in a copy field; against the brief (`--brief`, default `source.path`): every Products bullet is in `products[]`, every merge token survives (an Offer token in `offer`), Offer None means `offer` is null, each named channel is present, a named send date leaves a `send_date` gap | the engine's per-channel requirements (placements), figure tracing across fields, the retired route plan, that a gap's owner is the right one (a person reads that). This check is the slimmed `brief_lint`; the engine's `brief_lint.js` stays SKIPPED because it validates the engine's `brief-schema.json` |
| strategist | `python3 crew/checks/strategist_check.py --output <run>/strategist.json` | `default_route_count` angles; every axis in `route-axes.json` and no two alike; `opening_module` in `module-library.json` and not a no-copy module; `exemplar_ref` null or an id in `exemplars.json`; two registers or more across the set; lead lines of at most 140 characters in sentence case; against `intake.json` beside the output (or `--intake`): the slug matches, an `urgency` angle needs an offer, a `proof` angle needs a quote in `must_say`, and the brief's `never_say` applies; no em dash, prohibited hit or figure in a lead line | whether an angle's frame is supportable by the fact base (a person judges that at the pick). No engine lint covers angles alone |
| email | `python3 crew/checks/email_check.py --output <run>/email.json` | modules in `MOD_LABEL`; every slot id belongs to its module (own or composed element slot); every string within `max_chars`; caps slots in caps; price and disclaimer slots hold tokens only; card products exist with display names; three subject pairs; exclamation marks at most one and none in a headline, subject or CTA; no em dash, prohibited hit or typed price | voice quality, exemplar regurgitation, the register gate set, the one accent word, seam collisions. Engine `copy_lint.js` SKIPPED: it reads the retired slate shape; backlog `crew-email` adapts the lint or the shape |
| paid | `python3 crew/checks/paid_check.py --output <run>/paid.json` | ad types from `{single_image, story}` without repeat; three distinct primary texts and headlines per set; `spec_confidence` stays `unverified`; no em dash, prohibited hit, typed price, rating, review count, award or best-seller line; exclamation marks at most one and none in a headline | Meta character limits (the pack has no `placement-specs.json`; backlog `paid-specs`), grounding of concepts in customer material. Engine `paid_lint.js` SKIPPED: it reads the retired slate shape and needs a grounding pool |
| editor | `python3 crew/checks/editor_check.py --output <run>/editor.json --run <run>` | every edit's path resolves in `<run>/<stage>.json`; `before` is the exact current string; `after` differs, carries no em dash, prohibited hit or new typed figure; one edit per path | that an edit improves the line (a person accepts it). No engine lint covers a diff |

The em dash rule is enforced three times on purpose: the schema's `pattern` on copy strings, the `scan_text` pass in every check, and `build/emdash_scan.py` over the repo.
